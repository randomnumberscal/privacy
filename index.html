Privacy Policy

App: Integrated AI:Detective story

Effective date: November 12, 2026

Contact: turkaloartur3@gmail.com

Integrated AI:Detective story is a fictional detective role-playing game. This policy explains what information the app processes when you play.

Information processed

Information stored on your device

The app stores your selected language and case progress on your device. This may include case completion and retry timing. You can remove this information by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Questions and AI responses

When you ask a suspect a question, the app sends your question, the selected case, your selected language, and a limited portion of the recent conversation to the game's server. The server forwards the information and fictional case context to Google Gemini to generate a response. The game does not require an account or your real name.

The game's server does not save conversation history in a persistent database. Google processes prompts and responses under the terms and privacy rules that apply to the Gemini API. Depending on the applicable service tier and your location, Google may use submitted prompts and responses to improve its services, and authorized human reviewers may review them. Do not include passwords, contact details, or other sensitive personal information in your questions.

Character speech

If character speech is enabled, the text of a suspect's response is sent to Google Gemini to generate speech audio. The app receives the generated audio and saves it in the app's cache on your device for playback. Cached files may remain until they are removed by the operating system, the app, or you. The app does not record or send microphone audio.

Network and service information

Cloudflare processes network requests to deliver and protect the game's server. The server uses the connecting IP address for short-term, in-memory request rate limiting. The developer does not write that IP address to a persistent game database. Cloudflare may process technical request and security information under its own privacy policy and service terms.

How information is used

Information described in this policy is used to save local game progress, provide AI-generated suspect dialogue and speech, prevent excessive automated requests, and keep the service working securely.

Service providers and sharing

The game shares the relevant question, limited recent conversation, fictional case context, selected language, and speech text with Google through the game's server so Gemini can provide dialogue and speech. Cloudflare provides server and network services. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

The game does not sell personal information, use advertising SDKs, or use third-party analytics.

Retention and deletion

The developer does not keep a persistent server-side conversation database. Local progress remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Generated speech files are stored in the app cache and may be removed by the operating system or when you clear the app's storage.

Google and Cloudflare may retain or process service information according to their own terms, policies, and applicable law. The developer cannot directly delete information held by those providers. For provider-held data, use the providers' privacy controls or contact channels.

Children's privacy

The game is intended for a teen and adult audience and is not designed for children under 13. The game does not knowingly ask children to provide personal information. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through the game, contact the developer using the email above.

Security

Requests between the app, the game's server, and Google use encrypted HTTPS connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. The game is designed to limit persistent server-side storage of player conversation data.

Your choices and privacy rights

Avoid including personal information in game questions. You can clear locally stored game data through your device settings. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, or delete personal information. For privacy requests concerning information controlled by the developer, contact the developer using the email above. Requests concerning information processed by Google or Cloudflare should be directed to those providers.
